## Comparison Matrix

| Factor | EU (MiFID II MTF) | US (Reg ATS) | Singapore (MAS RMO) | UAE (VARA/ADGM) |
|--------|--------------------|--------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Regulatory framework** | MiFID II / MiFIR | Reg ATS / FINRA | SFA / MAS guidelines | VARA / ADGM framework |
| **License type** | MTF authorization | Form ATS-N registration | RMO license | VASP license / FSP |
| **Licensing timeline (est.)** | 12–18 months | 6–12 months | 6–12 months | 3–6 months |
| **Clock sync requirement** | ≤ 100 µs (PTP required) | ≤ 50 ms (NTP acceptable) | Per MAS guidance | Per framework |
| **Pre-trade transparency** | Required (with waivers) | Required (ATS-N) | Required | Required |
| **Post-trade transparency** | Required (MiFIR) | Required (SIP) | Required | Required |
| **Transaction reporting** | MiFIR Art.26 to ARM | CAT reporting | Per MAS | Per framework |
| **Surveillance obligation** | MAR compliance | FINRA oversight | MAS supervision | VARA supervision |
| **Data retention** | 5–7 years | 6 years (Rule 17a-4) | 5 years | 5 years |
| **Resilience testing** | RTS 7 mandatory | Reg SCI | MAS guidelines | Per framework |
| **Algo trading controls** | RTS 6 mandatory | Expected | Expected | Expected |
| **Position limits** | MiFID II (derivatives) | CFTC (derivatives) | Per asset class | Per framework |
| **Best suited asset classes** | Equities, bonds, derivatives, carbon (ETS) | Equities, ETFs, fixed income | Multi-asset, digital | Digital assets |
| **Participant pool size** | Very large (EU-wide) | Very large (US) | Moderate (APAC) | Growing |
| **Competitive landscape** | Very competitive (50+ venues) | Very competitive (40+ ATSs) | Moderate | Low |
| **Regulatory relationship cost** | High (legal + advisory) | High (FINRA + legal) | Moderate | Lower |

## Key Questions per Jurisdiction

1. Can we file the license application concurrently with FR-1 build?
2. Do we have existing regulatory advisory relationships?
3. What is the minimum capital requirement?
4. Are there local presence / staffing requirements?
5. Is the regulatory framework stable or rapidly evolving?
